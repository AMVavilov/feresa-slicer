Feresa Slicer brings a practical 3D-printing workflow to Android.

Import STL, OBJ, or 3MF models, arrange them on the build plate, configure real Orca-compatible print profiles, slice locally, inspect the layer-by-layer G-code toolpath, save the result, or send it to a Moonraker or OctoPrint printer.

Core features:
• local on-device slicing without an account;
• multiple models on one build plate;
• position, rotation, scaling, lay-flat, duplication, and auto-arrangement tools;
• built-in OrcaSlicer printer and process profiles;
• detailed print, filament, and printer settings;
• G-code preview by layer, feature type, speed, width, and layer height;
• optional read-only OrcaCloud profile synchronization;
• optional direct upload and guarded print start through Moonraker or OctoPrint;
• Russian and English interface with light and dark themes.
