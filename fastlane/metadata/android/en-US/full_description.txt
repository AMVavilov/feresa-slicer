Feresa Slicer brings local FDM slicing to Android.

Import STL, OBJ, or 3MF models, arrange one or more models on the build plate, choose printer, filament, and process profiles, slice on the device, inspect the G-code toolpath layer by layer, save the result, or upload it to a printer through Moonraker or OctoPrint.

Core features:
• local on-device slicing without an account;
• multiple models on one build plate;
• position, rotation, scaling, lay-flat, duplication, and auto-arrangement tools;
• built-in printer and process profiles derived from OrcaSlicer's AGPL-licensed system presets;
• detailed print, filament, and printer settings;
• G-code preview by layer, feature type, speed, width, and layer height;
• optional read-only OrcaCloud profile synchronization;
• optional G-code upload and print start through Moonraker or OctoPrint after a printer-status check;
• Russian and English interface with light and dark themes.
